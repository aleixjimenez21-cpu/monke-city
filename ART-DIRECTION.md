# RichMonke illustrated edition — asset briefs

All five final raster assets were produced with the built-in image-generation tool, inspected, and compressed as WebP. No fallback CLI was used. Existing gameplay source was preserved.

- `public/assets/art/skyline.webp`: panoramic premium cartoon blue/purple night skyline; original elegant skyscraper with a large luminous green crown around 70% of the composition; gold windows, layered distant architecture, moon, clouds and water reflections; no UI.
- `public/assets/art/buildings.webp`: cohesive wide side-elevation strip containing shabby Monke Motel on the left, warmly lit green Monke Stop in the middle and purple Night Owl on the right. Detailed balconies, doors, signs, pipes, windows, weeds and props. Transparent background. A targeted follow-up removed an incorrectly baked checkerboard while preserving the buildings and composition.
- `public/assets/art/npcs.webp`: six full-body transparent cartoon NPCs in two rows: skeptical thinner Doubter in dark jacket with folded arms, seated phone character, friendly older shopkeeper with apron; worker with hardhat, suited business character, blue-clad delivery character. Shared RichMonke anatomy and outline/shading language, distinct clothing and silhouettes.
- `public/assets/art/richmonke-poses.webp`: nine transparent right-facing game poses, idle / walk A / walk B / run A / run B / airborne / landing / interaction / thinking. Preserve brown fur, orange/tan face, black-framed green glasses, yellow shorts; no chain, watch or jewelry. Exact cropped bounds are in `game/art.ts`.
- `public/assets/art/interior.webp`: premium cartoon panoramic side-scrolling Monke Stop interior; warm amber lamps, cream brick, scuffed honey wood floor, blue/purple nighttime through left door, shelves of banana snacks, drinks, coffee and magazines, counter and register, no people or UI. Open foreground floor for movement.

Keep all three buildings, motel signage, palm tree, pipes, wires, all details unchanged. Exact same wide composition and building positions. Output PNG with true transparent background, NOT checkerboard pixels. Do not add shadows outside buildings. This is a game sprite strip.”
